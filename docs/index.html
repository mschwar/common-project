Welcome

Imagine understanding complex scientific ideas as easily as you understand why ice melts or why balls roll downhill. That's what we're building here: a collection of 50 profound concepts from statistics, biology, evolution, systems thinking, and more—all explained like you're five years old.

Inspired by Feynman's teaching style and classic explainer videos, each entry breaks down PhD-level ideas into everyday analogies. No jargon. No lecturing. Just curiosity-driven learning that builds your mental toolkit.
