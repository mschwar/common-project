Cellular Automata

Neighbors determine survival. Complex life emerges from empty space.

The Rules Are Simple

Every cell follows two rules:

  1. Survival: A living cell with 2-3 neighbors survives; otherwise it dies.
  2. Birth: An empty cell with exactly 3 neighbors becomes alive.

That's it. From these two rules, infinite complexity emerges.
